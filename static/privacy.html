Privacy & Data Protection Policy

· Last updated

This policy explains how (“we”, “the Firm”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal data of our clients, their representatives and our staff, and the rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. You may ask for this notice in English or any language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Firm is the Data Fiduciary: it decides why and how your personal data is processed and is responsible for it under the DPDP Act.

2. Personal data we process

  • Identity and contact details — name, PAN, GSTIN, TAN, address, phone, email, authorised contact person; for minors, the parent or lawful guardian.
  • Financial and tax records you give us — bank statements, sales and purchase registers, invoices, GST returns and statements (e.g. GSTR-2B), salary registers, TDS/TCS challans, Form 16, AIS / 26AS, investment and deduction proofs, capital-gains statements, books of account, financial statements, board and AGM documents.
  • Records of our work — filing status, due dates, acknowledgement / ARN numbers, our notes.
  • Communications — messages and files you send us by WhatsApp, email or upload.
  • Our staff — name, work email, phone, login credentials (stored only as a secure hash) and activity logs.

Please do not send Aadhaar numbers or biometric data unless a filing specifically requires it; where a document contains them incidentally, we use them only for that filing.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

We process personal data only for these purposes:

    We rely on your consent (DPDP Act s.6), given after you receive this notice, and — where the law permits — on legitimate uses under s.7, including complying with obligations under tax, GST and company law. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale.

    4. How it is processed

    • Files you send are sorted automatically by client, document type and period. Anything the software is not sure about is checked by a person; no decision with legal effect on you is taken by software alone.
    • Every filing is reviewed and approved by a partner before it is filed, and recorded with its acknowledgement.
    • Access inside the Firm is limited per person; staff can be restricted to the clients they handle.

    5. Who we share it with

      We never sell personal data. Some of these service providers may process data outside India; such transfers are made only as permitted under s.16 of the DPDP Act.

      6. How long we keep it

      We keep financial and tax records for years after the end of the financial year they relate to, because tax, GST and company laws and professional standards require records to be retained for audit, assessment and inspection. After that, or once you withdraw consent and no law requires us to keep them, they are erased. Security logs are kept for at least one year as required by the DPDP Rules.

      7. How we protect it

      • Individual logins for every staff member, passwords stored only as salted hashes, automatic lock-out after repeated failed sign-ins, and access limited by role and by client.
      • Access and changes are logged; logs are kept for at least one year.
      • Regular backups, and an incident procedure for personal data breaches.

      8. Your rights

      Under the DPDP Act you can ask us, free of charge:

      • Access (s.11) — a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we process it and with whom it was shared.
      • Correction, completion, updating (s.12) of inaccurate or incomplete data.
      • Erasure (s.12) of data no longer needed — except records the law requires us to keep, which we will tell you about.
      • Withdraw consent (s.6(4)) at any time, as easily as you gave it. We will stop processing, except where the law requires us to keep records. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and we may then be unable to continue providing services.
      • Grievance redressal (s.13) — we respond within days.
      • Nominate (s.14) a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

      Write to the Grievance Officer above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process.

      Under s.15 you also have duties: do not give false information, impersonate another person, or file frivolous complaints.

      9. Children

      Where a client is under 18 (for example, a minor with income or investments), we process their data only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, and never for tracking, behavioural monitoring or advertising.

      10. Personal data breaches

      If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, with a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours, describing what happened, its likely consequences, what we have done and what you can do.

      11. Cookies

      This site uses a single, strictly necessary cookie to keep staff signed in. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

      12. Changes

      We will update this page when our practices change and tell clients about material changes.
